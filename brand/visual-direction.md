# Visual Direction

## Intent

Vehicle Transport Australia should feel like a national vehicle logistics
brand: direct, practical, quote-led, and confident across long-distance
transport routes.

The quote subdomain should carry the same dark transport shell and orange
conversion cues as `cartransport.au`, while keeping Moveroo's imported quote
flow fast, readable, and mobile-first.

## Core Look

- **Mood:** national, capable, road-and-route focused, clear, high-contrast.
- **Palette:** deep slate and near-black shells, white form surfaces, vivid
  transport orange, and a small amber highlight for energy.
- **Primary shell:** `#0f172a`.
- **Deep shell:** `#020617`.
- **Primary action orange:** `#f97316`.
- **Strong action orange:** `#ea580c`.
- **Warm highlight:** `#f59e0b`.
- **Typography:** Roboto, with bold direct headings and plain operational form
  labels.
- **Mark:** simple orange VTA block mark from `public/logo.svg`.
- **Motifs:** car transport routes, depot coverage, interstate city pairs,
  vehicle carriers, and Australia-wide movement cues.

## What To Borrow From The Current Site

- Sticky dark header with orange brand accent.
- Vehicle Transport / Australia wordmark treatment.
- Dark hero shell with subtle route/industrial patterning.
- White instant-quote card with an orange top accent.
- Orange primary CTAs with compact rounded-rectangle shape.
- Service cards on light panels and transport data panels on dark slate.
- Customer copy focused on safe, secure, affordable vehicle movement.

## What To Avoid

- Do not copy Backloading Removals black/red.
- Do not reuse another vehicle site's blue/teal visual profile.
- Do not copy the Astro homepage layout wholesale into Moveroo.
- Do not make a full marketing hero block push the first quote input below the
  fold on mobile.
- Do not put route patterns, truck imagery, or map motifs behind form controls.
- Do not make household removals the primary journey for this brand.
- Do not show admin or provider links on public customer surfaces.
- Do not expose direct or placeholder phone values.

## Reusable Asset Families

- `logo`: orange VTA mark from `public/logo.svg`.
- `favicon`: matching compact VTA/favicon mark.
- `route`: diagonal route/road striping or city-pair lines.
- `vehicle`: small car-carrier or vehicle movement cues outside the form body.
- `coverage`: Australia-wide depot/city list treatment.

## Approval Checklist

- The surface reads as Vehicle Transport Australia or CarTransport.au before a
  customer reaches the form.
- Vehicle quote is the primary journey.
- The first form step remains reachable quickly on mobile.
- CTAs use orange/amber over accessible dark or white surfaces.
- Form fields stay light and calm even when the surrounding shell is dark.
- Customer portal links are present but low-prominence.
- Household quote, booking, contact, and customer login routes remain available.
- Provider and admin links remain disabled.
